# The transport bar {#transport-bar}

The bar along the top of the window controls playback and settings for the whole song. From left to right:

| Control                   | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **rebeat** + project name | Click the name to open the [project browser](command:project.home). A small dot next to it means there are changes that haven't been saved yet (they save automatically within a second).                                                                                                                          |
| **Play / Stop**           | Starts and stops playback ({{key:transport.toggle}}). Playback starts at the section you're editing. Stop is silent at once: no reverb or delay tails, no fading notes (unless **Let effects ring out after stop** is on in [Settings](#settings)).                                                                |
| **Rec**                   | Record ({{key:transport.record}}). While playing, notes and pads you play are written into the pattern. With an armed Clip track, it records a loop. See [Recording notes](#instruments-recording-notes) and [The loop station](#loop-station).                                                                    |
| **Ovd / Rep**             | **Overdub** adds notes and keeps what's there. **Replace** clears a step the first time you play onto it in each loop pass. See [Recording notes](#instruments-recording-notes).                                                                                                                                   |
| **Loop**                  | How long a loop recording is: the pattern length, or 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **1.1.1**                 | Where the playhead is: bar . beat . step.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **BPM**                   | Tempo. Drag up/down or scroll to change it; double-click to reset to 120. Hold Shift while dragging for fine steps.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Tap**                   | Tap a few times in time with the music to set the tempo ({{key:transport.tap}}).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **4/4**                   | Time signature: 4/4, 3/4, 5/4, 6/8 or 7/8.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Click**                 | Metronome on/off ({{key:transport.metronome}}). The click isn't recorded or exported.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **1234**                  | Count-in: one bar of clicks (or two, see [Settings](#settings)) before playback or recording starts from a stop.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Swing**                 | Swing for the whole song: 50% is straight, higher values delay every second step. Patterns and tracks can have their own swing.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Magnet                    | **Snap**: when on, recorded note starts and lengths snap to the Q grid. When off, the exact timing is kept as a sub-step offset and heard on playback. Also controls free dragging in the piano roll (Alt overrides).                                                                                              |
| **Q**                     | The grid size for live recording (1/4, 1/8, 1/16 or 1/32). Also used by **Quantize selection** (Ctrl/Cmd+Q) in the piano roll.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Loop / Song**           | **Loop** repeats the section you're on; **Song** plays the sections in order, with their repeats ({{key:transport.mode}}). New projects start in Song; the choice is saved with the project.                                                                                                                       |
| **CPU**                   | The worst of three load signals: audio render load ("Audio"), main-thread busy fraction ("Main"), or scheduler tick lateness ("Sched"). The label says which signal is shown. Hover the meter for the full breakdown. If it stays high you may hear glitches — close other tabs or lower the UI scale in Settings. |
| Level meter               | The level of the master output (left and right).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Audio status              | Green when sound is running.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ⌘                         | The [command palette](command:app.palette).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Layout button             | Switch between layout presets and show any panel. See [Panels and layouts](#panels).                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Full screen               | The whole app full screen ({{key:view.fullscreen}}). Esc leaves full screen.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **?**                     | This guide.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Speech bubble             | **Explain mode** ({{key:app.explain}}): hover over any button for a card that explains it, with its shortcut. See [Explain mode](#getting-started-explain-mode).                                                                                                                                                   |
| Gear                      | [Settings](command:app.settings) ({{key:app.settings}}).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

On narrow windows some controls hide; they're always available in the command palette.

## The keys {#keys-target}

The **Keys** chip in the transport bar shows what the keys play: by default it follows the selected track (the chip reads "Keys · _track name_"). Click it to open a menu:

- **Follow selected track** — the keys always play whatever track you click on in the drum machine. This is the default.
- **A specific track** — the keys stay on that track even when you select a different one. Useful when you want to play a bass line while editing the drums above it.
- **A library sound** — the keys play a sound from the library without adding it to the project. The sound plays through a **Keys** strip in the mixer (it appears while a library sound is active), so you can add reverb and delay just like a track. Recent library sounds appear at the bottom of the menu.

To put a library sound on the keys from the library panel: hover over it and click the keyboard icon, or press **K** with it selected. You can then play it with your MIDI keyboard, the on-screen pads, or the computer keys.

**Add as a track** — if you've been playing a library sound and want to keep it, click "Add as a track" in the Keys menu (or run **Add the keys' sound as a track** from the command palette). Rebeat adds a Notes track with that sound and points the keys at it.

**Follow the synth editor in focus** — tick it in the Keys menu and the keys play what the synth editor panel in focus edits: the track of a synth editor tab, or a library synth you opened from the library (it plays that sound's own voice, so you hear your edits as you play). With several editors open, the one you clicked last is in focus; close it and the keys play their usual target again. While it's on, it's the only choice ticked in the menu; picking another one turns it off.

The top of the Keys menu says exactly what plays and where it comes from ("Acid Bass", "Synth editor · BASS", "Track BASS" or "Library"), the chip reads **Keys · Editor** while it follows an editor, and a MiniLab 3 shows the same two lines on its display.

**Muted and turned-down tracks still play from the keys.** The keys have their own channel per track, with the track's sound and effects: you hear what you play even when the track is muted (or another one is soloed), at full level (0 dB) whatever its fader says. While you're recording, it plays at the track's level, the way it will play back.

**Samples play from the keys too**, on a Hits track (a kick, a snare…) as well as from the library: the sample's base key plays it as it is (its detected pitch, else C4), higher keys play it higher, lower keys lower. A sample plays to its end like a pad, so a short tap plays the whole kick; a long sample (over 2 seconds) stops when you let go. On a Hits track, recording writes a hit.

The keys always use a separate voice from the sequencer. On a mono synth the sequencer keeps playing uninterrupted while you play on top of it.
