# Patterns and the song {#patterns}

A song in Rebeat is a row of **sections** (an intro, a verse, a fill…). Each section plays a **pattern**: the steps of every track. Tracks, their sounds and their mixer settings are the same in every pattern.

- A **pattern** is what the drum machine edits: its steps, its length, step size, swing, key and name.
- A **section** is an entry in the song: it plays a pattern, as often as its repeat count.

## Copies and clones

- A **copy** is a new section with its own copy of the pattern. Change it freely.
- A **clone** is a section that shares its pattern with another one (linked): editing either one changes both. Clones show a chain icon and share an outline color. **Unlink** (right-click the section) turns a clone into a copy.

Make them from the section's right-click menu (**Copy section**, **Clone section (linked)**), with COPY or DUPL + SHIFT, or by Alt-dragging (copy) and Alt+Shift-dragging (clone) a section in the song strip.

## Length and timing per pattern

Each pattern has its own number of **steps** and **step size**, so a pattern can be half a bar, one bar or several bars, in straight or triplet steps. **×2** doubles a pattern and copies its steps into the new half; **÷2** (SHIFT + ×2) halves it. Patterns can also have their own **swing**, **key**, and a **transpose** for the tracks that play notes.

Within a pattern, a track can have its **own length** (it loops sooner, for polyrhythms) and its **own step size** (for example a 1/32 hi-hat over a 1/16 pattern): right-click the track name.

## Playing a song

- In **Loop** mode, the section you're on repeats.
- **Song** is the default, and the mode you pick is saved with the project.
- In **Song** mode, the sections play in order, each as often as its repeat count (right-click → Repeat). After the last section the song starts over, unless you switch looping off with the loop button next to **Loop / Song**.
- While playing, **click a section to queue it**: it starts at the end of the current section, or on the next bar or beat if you choose so in [Settings](#settings).
- **Follow** (header bar) makes the drum machine show whichever section is playing.
