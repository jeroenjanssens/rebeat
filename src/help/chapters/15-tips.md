# Tips and troubleshooting {#tips}

## No sound

- Click **Click to start audio** if it's showing, and check that the dot at the right of the transport bar is green.
- Check the master level meter while playing. If it moves but you hear nothing, check **Settings → Audio → Output device** and your computer's volume.
- A track is silent when it's muted (**M**), when another track is soloed (**S**), or when its fader is all the way down.

## The microphone doesn't work

- Allow microphone access when the browser asks. If you said no, allow it in the browser's site settings (the icon next to the address).
- Pick the right **Input device** in Settings → Audio.
- Recordings land late? Run **Calibrate…** in Settings → Audio.

## Glitches or dropouts

- Set **Latency** to Balanced or Safe in Settings → Audio, and reload.
- Close panels you don't need; scopes and big waveforms take some work to draw.
- Many tracks with reverbs are heavy: use the shared **Reverb** send instead of a reverb on every track.

## Where is my work?

In this browser, saved automatically. Use **Export .rebeat** to keep a copy elsewhere or move it to another computer. Clearing the browser's site data deletes it; **persistent storage** (Settings → Storage) protects it from being cleared automatically.

## Handy habits

- Hover over a button to see what it does and its shortcut.
- Press {{key:app.palette}} and type what you want to do.
- Right-click almost anything (a track name, a page, a pad, a sample, a clip, a knob) for more options.
- Undo is always there: {{key:edit.undo}}.
